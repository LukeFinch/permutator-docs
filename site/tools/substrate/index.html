Substrate

Nested components land on the right surface by themselves.

Annotate components with an elevation offset from the surface beneath them, or a fixed level. Pick the surface a selection sits on and apply. Every annotated layer inside gets its matching surface mode.

Why it helpsCards in panels in sheets end up on the right surface however deep they nest, and nobody picks modes by hand.

on surface-2 → card +1 = surface-3 · popover +2 = surface-5
